      My roster

      Pick your base roster and a starting week (and which line that week is). New timesheets are then filled in for you: shifts as rostered, rest days boxed off.

      Road shifts are from Sligo, Manorhamilton or Ballyshannon. Choose "Ask each time" if it varies — each Road day then asks.

      When your entered shifts run out, the app asks for the next lot before you start a timesheet. Leave you've already requested, or put in with a form, is added on top.

      Leave rules

      Some leave pays a standard day rather than your full shift. On a longer shift the app asks how the rest is covered (Annual Leave, TOIL or worked). Check these with HR. You can change them any time.
